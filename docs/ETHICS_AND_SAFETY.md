# Ethics and Safety / Ética y seguridad

## Core rules

1. **Only test what you own or have explicit written permission to test.**
2. **Keep labs isolated.** Use host-only/internal networks. Do not bridge vulnerable VMs to the Internet or your real LAN.
3. **No malware distribution.** Do not store or publish live malicious samples. Use inert, synthetic, or legally obtained training artifacts.
4. **Document everything.** Record scope, snapshots, and actions.
5. **Stop on uncertainty.** If you are not sure an action is authorized or safe, do not do it.

## Why isolation matters

A misconfigured lab can expose vulnerable services, leak credentials, or let test artifacts reach other machines. Isolation protects you, your network, and others.

## Legal note

Laws vary by country, but unauthorized access, interception, or disruption of computer systems is commonly illegal. This repository is for defensive education only.
